# Hamburger helper with bacon today

Using some of leftover bacon from the chicken bacon pizza from the other day I made some Hamburger Helper with ground Turkey cooked and seasoned with the same blend as above, using a box of Great Value cheesy hamburger macaroni and then topped it with 4 chopped up pieces of bacon that I crisped in a second skillet, that I then decided to mix completely into the pasta. It's pretty damn delicious but not exactly what I'd cook for my date. The only way I think I deviated from box instructions for this is I do think it's better cook the ground Turkey to the point that the water is removed and you get some browning of the ground Turkey by pushing it past that, to get that better flavor from the browning, rather than what the box says, which is to cook and then drain the excess grease before adding the water, milk, pasta and cheese mix. I've also cooked this many times with browning an onion with the cooked meat, but I've stopped doing that because these hamburger helper skillet meals are typically rich enough for my stomach without an onion. Another thing I've done a few times but not this time is adding more pasta and water to the recipe.
